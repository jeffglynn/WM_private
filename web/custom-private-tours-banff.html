    WINTER ITINERARY 1: Private Guided 1/2 Day Kananaskis Touring Experience

    Driving tour with brief stops along the way to explore, play and take in the unspoiled mountain views Travel through a remote rugged winter wilderness featuring snowcapped peaks, endless pine forests and frozen lakes

    Potential activities include:

    • Experience snowshoeing right from your vehicle
    • ‘Wild Ice’: Skate on a remote mountain lake (conditional on snow/ice conditions)
    • Nature photography: stunning up-close mountain views in all directions, spot wildlife including big horn sheep and mountain goats
    • Enjoy hot chocolate and cookies fireside
    • Finish your day with a charcuterie experience at a backcountry lodge
    • Enjoy the neighbouring mountain town of Canmore’s picturesque downtown for shopping, restaurants, microbreweries/distillery
    • Drive the remote and rugged Smith Dorian Roadway
    • Winter sleigh rides (additional cost applies)

    Tour includes private interpretive guide, return transportation from Banff/Canmore, snowshoe rental, hot chocolate & cookies

    Ice skate rental, sleigh ride, charcuterie available for additional fee, please ask at time of booking

    Total time from Banff: 5 hours

    Total driving time: 2.5 hours return approx.

    ​​

    WINTER ITINERARY 2: Private Guided 1/2 Day Lake Louise Experience

    Enjoy a tour to Lake Louise with ample time to partake in some truly Canadian winter experiences. At Lake Louise you can walk along the frozen lakeshore, join your guide for a bit of snowshoeing, rent some ice skates and skate on the lake, or take a horse drawn sleigh ride across the lake and back. If you prefer to look at the incredible mountain scenery from the comfort of a warm chair, why not enjoy some lunch or tea inside the Chateau Lake Louise.

    Potential activities include:

    • Experience snowshoeing at Lake Louise (snowshoes included)
    • Ice skating on Lake Louise (rentals available in advance or at Chateau Sports in Lake Louise)
    • Try a guided icewalk in a frozen canyon enroute
    • Enjoy hot chocolate and cookies fireside
    • Enjoy lunch or afternoon tea at Chateau Lake Louise (ask us for reservations and pricing options)
    • Drive along the Bow Valley Parkway
    • Winter sleigh rides at Lake Louise (additional cost applies)

    • Tour includes private interpretive guide, return transportation from Banff/Canmore, snowshoe rental, hot chocolate & cookies Ice skate rental, sleigh ride, charcuterie available for additional fee, please ask at time of booking

      Total time from Banff: 5 hours

      Total driving time (from Banff): 2 hours return approx.

    ​​

    WINTER ITINERARY 3: Private Guided 1/2 Day Kootenay Park Explorer

    A short drive from Banff  into neighbouring Kootenay National Park in British Columbia. Here take in the stunning mountain scenery, with options for a canyon icewalk or a snowshoeing through peaceful pine forests to reach a secluded frozen lake. Options for lunch at a remote backcountry lodge or finish your day with a soak in the hot springs also available.

    Potential activities include:

    • Experience snowshoeing at the Continental Divide (snowshoes included)
    • Try a guided icewalk in a frozen canyon enroute
    • Enjoy hot chocolate and cookies fireside
    • Enjoy Charucterie at a back country lodge (ask us for reservations and pricing options)
    • Drive along the Kootenay Parkway
    • Experience Radium Hotsprings 

    • Tour includes private interpretive guide, return transportation from Banff/Canmore, snowshoe rental, hot chocolate & cookies Hotsprings entrance, charcuterie available for additional fee, please ask at time of booking

      Total time from Banff: 5 hours

      Total driving time (from Banff): 2 hours return approx.
